Who Reads, Who Decides: The Deterministic Triad

Unlike consumer generative tools that synthesize answers through statistical probability, SachCheck divides ingestion, retrieval, and judgment into three hermetically isolated components.

The Core SachCheck Axiom

AI reads. Evidence proves. Rules decide.

balance

AI reads the message and sources

Ingests unstructured forwards: audio voicenotes, WhatsApp screenshot OCR, and vernacular Hindi, Marathi, and Tamil dialects. Extracts discrete, testable factual propositions.

Scope: Token Extraction ONLY

Cryptographic string comparison matches claims against primary gazettes, Supreme Court judgments, and RBI repository records. A citation is rejected if its URL hash fails integrity audits.

Scope: Exact Cryptographic Match
03

Fixed rules choose the verdict

No machine learning model ever issues a verdict. A deterministic, immutable statutory precedence table maps the gathered citations to one of exactly five verdicts.

Scope: Deterministic Logic Engine

Why the Large Language Model is forbidden from making decisions

When language models are allowed to "guess" veracity, they suffer from sycophancy, subtle geographic hallucination, and bias toward sensational phrasing. Under SachCheck protocol, if our deterministic lookup finds no matching statutory or archival text, the system defaults to "Cannot Be Confirmed". The AI is legally prevented from fabricating connective tissue.

Where We Look: The Evidence Source Ladder

Search order is entirely distinct from evidential weighting. While search crawlers index web links indiscriminately, our adjudication engine applies strict institutional precedence.

TIER 1

Official & Statutory Public Records

verified 100% Precedence Weight

The Gazette of India, Supreme Court & High Court signed orders, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulatory circulars, and departmental notifications published on official .gov.in domains.

“A Tier 1 statutory record automatically overrides all secondary press analyses or social assertions.”
TIER 2

Certified Independent Fact-Checkers

High Corroborative Weight

Signatories of the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) operating across India (e.g., BoomLive, AltNews, Factly, Vishvas News) and institutional civic verification desks (PIB Fact Check).

Requires two mutually independent signatories to achieve statutory consensus if Tier 1 text is pending publication.
TIER 3

Established News Wire & Primary Reporting

Secondary Corroborative

Press Trust of India (PTI), United News of India, The Hindu, Indian Express, and accredited parliamentary correspondents. Used exclusively to substantiate ground events, localized incidents, and minister statements.

TIER 4

Peer-Reviewed & Academic Repositories

Supplementary Context

ICMR scientific advisories, IIT engineering audits, university journals, and institutional whitepapers. Provides scientific baseline for medical or technical viral claims.

TIER 5

Social Feeds, Viral Blogs & WhatsApp Broadcasts

block 0% Evidential Weight

Viral forward chains, anonymous blog pages, YouTube talking heads, and unverified social media handles. Used strictly as ingestion artifacts to define the claim; never accepted as proof.

verified Section 03 · Standardized Adjudication

Five Standardized Verdicts

Every claim submitted to SachCheck terminates in exactly one of these five verdicts. We do not use ambiguous scores, star ratings, or percentage probabilities.

verified_user VERIFIED
Criterion: Primary Statutory Record Matched

The claim is completely substantiated by authentic, unexpired Tier 1 statutory records, official ministry decrees, or certified institutional registries.

Historical Case Example:
“Union Cabinet sanctions ₹23,136 crore for Mumbai Metro Line 3 extension under Phase 2.”

Matched directly with Press Information Bureau (PIB) Cabinet Decision Release & Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs notification.

cancel FALSE
Criterion: Directly Contradicts Statutory Record

The central assertion has no grounding in fact, relies on doctored materials, or directly contradicts public records and binding laws.

Historical Case Example:
“RBI declares that ₹500 notes where the green security thread is close to Mahatma Gandhi's photo are fake.”

Directly refuted by official Reserve Bank of India notification DCM(Plg) No. G-2/2016 clarifying both variations are valid currency prints.

history OUTDATED
Criterion: Superseded Statutory Order

The forwarded text was accurate at a previous moment in history, but has since lapsed, expired, or been superseded by newer legislative or administrative amendments.

Historical Case Example:
“PAN-Aadhaar linking deadline extended without penalty fee to June 30.”

Legitimate circular from 2021 recirculated in 2025 where statutory penalty rules under Section 234H of the Income Tax Act are now mandatory.

timelapse PARTLY SUPPORTED
Criterion: Factual Kernel with Distorted Metrics

Contains a genuine factual premise, but has been distorted with inflated figures, fabricated qualifying criteria, or misleading conditional constraints.

Historical Case Example:
“Government to provide ₹50,000 direct benefit transfer scholarship to all Class 10 female students nationwide.”

The scholarship scheme exists under the Begum Hazrat Mahal program, but pays ₹5,000 for classes 9-10 and requires minority community income criteria.

help_outline CANNOT BE CONFIRMED
Criterion: Insufficient Primary Audit Trail

There is currently no official verifiable public documentation, gazette notice, police charge sheet, or institutional record to either confirm or falsify the claim.

Historical Case Example:
“Municipal commissioner in Vidarbha secretly directed water tankers away from agricultural zones last weekend.”

No official municipal dispatch, logging log, or on-record district collector confirmation exists. We refuse to speculate.

balance Section 05 · The Ethics of Restraint

When We Abstain: Restraint as Institutional Duty

Commercial fact-checking often feels pressured to deliver binary answers. We reject that pressure categorically.

“Cannot Be Confirmed” is an honest result, not a failure.

When a civic rumor spreads about an unlogged local panchayat meeting, a closed-door bureaucratic transfer, or a private phone conversation between politicians, an automated service that issues a confident “False” is lying to citizens. You cannot prove a negative without complete record access.

By delivering “Cannot Be Confirmed”, SachCheck preserves democratic vigilance. We explicitly state: “No public record exists. Do not act on this forward until official gazettes or institutional dispatches emerge.”

What We Cannot Do: Explicit Institutional Boundaries

To maintain credibility, a civic institution must state its technical boundaries plainly. SachCheck does not overpromise.

image_not_supported

No Image Tampering Detection

We check the claims, statements, and numbers present within an image. We do NOT run pixel-level Error Level Analysis (ELA) or promise forensic proof of digital Photoshop manipulation.

Audits semantic content, not compression artifacts
smart_toy

No Deepfake Forensic Guarantee

Generative video and synthetic audio evolve daily. We do not certify videos as “real” or “synthetic”; we audit whether the speech transcript matches signed press dispatches or transcripts.

Transcript match only; zero synthetic biometric claims
translate

Dialect Beta Operational Tier

While Standard Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, and English are fully supported in production, regional rural idioms and mixed Hinglish voice notes are in continuous monitored beta.

Dialect ingestion reliability tracked openly
